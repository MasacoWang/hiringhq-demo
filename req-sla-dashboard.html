Pre-Hire / Hired detail rule

Do not rely on the My Requisitions homepage for Pre-Hire or Hired. Open each req detail page and read the top status row counts. If Pre-Hire > 0, check disposition risk. If Hired > 0, alert to close the req in EC.

Feedback path

For Business Review, open Review tab and choose the Business Review view only. Business Review includes Pending + Completed; exclude GTA Review. Business Review column should show only the decision: Yes - Advance to Screen, No - not qualified, or Pending.
