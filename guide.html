Dashboard views

1

Hiring Progress Overview

· Req-level

See the phase & progress of all your reqs at a glance.

What needs attention?Pipeline status is scattered across TA Hub — checking every req one by one is slow, and stalled reqs or idle candidates are easy to miss.

Pre-Approval Business Review Phone Screen Interview Offer Pre-Hire Hired

Covers the full req lifecycle: Pre-Approval → Hired

  • Funnel stats: Active / Offering / Pre-Hire / Hired / No activity
  • Per-req phase, latest feedback, upcoming interview, referral source
  • Filter columns, sort headers, click through to candidate / req

ImpactSingle source of truth for pipeline health. Spot stalled reqs and idle candidates in seconds — fewer things slip, faster time-to-fill, and every HM update starts from live data instead of digging through TA Hub.

Source daily-report-table.html · Use when starting the day or prepping an HM update

2

Feedback + Schedule

· Candidate-level

A clear view of each candidate's interviews & feedback, side by side.

What is blocking progress?Feedback and scheduling live in different places, so candidates silently stall — an interview happens but feedback never comes, or feedback is in but the next round is never booked.

Business Review Phone Screen Interview

Covers the active pipeline: Business Review → Interview

  • Feedback status and interview schedule on the same row
  • Spots gaps: "interview booked, feedback missing" or "feedback in, next round unscheduled"
  • Flags schedule & feedback pending — who's waiting on a slot or a decision
  • Filter & sort every column; click through to the candidate

ImpactKeeps candidates moving — no dead air. Catches missing feedback and unscheduled next rounds before they stall a loop, protecting candidate experience and stopping good people from dropping out while they wait.

Source combined-feedback-schedule-dashboard.html · Use when chasing feedback or checking scheduling

3

TA Hub Req SLA

· Risk & timeliness

Follow up on SLA risk for operational excellence.

What is at risk?Aging applications and post-offer clean-up (disposition, EC close) are easy to lose track of — breaches surface only after they've already hurt SLA metrics and candidate experience.

  • New App aging: 7–13d and ≥14d (mutually exclusive)
  • Req age bands: 30–59 / 60–89 / ≥90 days
  • 5-day disposition after OA · close the req in EC once Hired

ImpactOperational excellence & SLA compliance. Surfaces aging applications and post-offer clean-up before they breach policy — protecting your SLA metrics, audit readiness, and a fair, timely process for every applicant.

Source SLA dashboard · Use when reviewing risk & setting daily priorities

4

Reports

· Email generators

Turn tracking into proactive, one-click communication — to yourself or per Hiring Manager.

What action should I take next?Writing status updates by hand is slow and reactive — HMs get late or inconsistent updates, decisions drag, and when there's no time for a daily review, things quietly stall with no one nudging them forward.

  • Daily · Recruiter — email yourself the whole-portfolio Hiring Progress Overview; optional auto-send each weekday.
  • Weekly · Hiring Manager — one click drafts every HM's tailored weekly update (Outlook draft or copy to you).
  • Auto interview-complete alert — the moment a req's full loop finishes, you're notified automatically to confirm next steps.
  • Fetch latest data — re-scrape first, or use data on disk.

Business impactTurns reports into action and impact. Instead of manually writing status updates, one click proactively drafts every HM's weekly report — recruiter-reviewed before sending — and pushes an instant alert when interviews wrap. This accelerates hiring decisions, removes hours of admin, and cuts recurring status meetings. Faster, proactive comms also mean a stronger candidate experience — shorter waits, timely next steps, and less risk of losing top talent to a slow process.

Nothing goes to an HM automatically — drafts and self-emails only; you stay in control.

5

Disposition Helper

· Opens in a new tab

For a candidate who failed the full-loop interview: recommend other roles & add to a Talent Pool.

How can I preserve talent value?Strong candidates who miss one role are often simply declined and lost — finding them a better-fit opening is manual and rarely happens, hurting candidate experience and wasting future sourcing effort.

  • Paste their Career Hub profile link → get better-matched open roles for the disposition email
  • Helps add the candidate to a suitable Talent Pool
  • Read-only & advisory — never submits the disposition or sends email for you

ImpactTurns rejections into a warm talent pipeline. A strong candidate who missed one role gets pointed to a better-fit opening and saved to a Talent Pool — improving candidate experience, protecting the employer brand, and cutting future sourcing time and cost.

Local tool (port 8766) · If offline, run Start Disposition Helper in the Sunny disposition folder
